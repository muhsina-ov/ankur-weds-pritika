# Ankur & Pritika — Wedding Invitation

> **Sadde ghar vyah hai!**  
> Two families. Five celebrations. One *dil khol ke* Punjabi wedding.

Digital wedding invitation for **Ankur Chauhan & Pritika Khanna**, celebrating on **04 December 2026** in New Delhi.

---

## 🌟 Features
- **Traditional Entrance Gate**: Realistic Punjabi courtyard reveal with interactive petal bursts.
- **Background Music (BGM)**: Festive wedding celebration track with mute/play toggle and audio management.
- **Interactive Wedding Itinerary**: Sufi Night, Patiala Night, Cocktail & Jaggo, Haldi, and Shaadi.
- **Save the Date & RSVP**: Instant calendar `.ics` download and 1-tap WhatsApp response.
- **Optimized Performance**: Pre-compressed WebP assets, zero Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), and async image decoding.
- **Rich Open Graph Metadata**: Custom 1200×630 couple caricature social share card for WhatsApp, iMessage, and Twitter previews.
