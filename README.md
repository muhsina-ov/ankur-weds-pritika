# Ankur & Pritika — Wedding Invitation

> **#AnkurKiPreeto**  
> Two families. Five celebrations. One grand Punjabi wedding.

Luxury digital wedding invitation for **Ankur Chauhan & Pritika Khanna**, celebrating on **04 December 2026** in New Delhi.

---

## 🌟 Structure & Features

### Part 1: Morning Royal Gate & Cinematic Welcome
- **Cinematic Palace Gate Opening**: High-definition Google Flow video entrance where palace doors swing open inward, revealing a sunlit haveli courtyard with zero-flicker crossfade into the page.
- **7-Star Royal Stationery**: Mughal arched card with 24K gold foil gradients, embossed Peacock AP crest medallion, and an emerald jewel CTA button with animated light shimmer.
- **Celebratory Music Synchronization**: Instagram reel audio track with an interactive frosted ivory equalizer controller.
- **Continuous Floating Petals**: Hardware-accelerated rose and marigold petals drifting gracefully down the screen.

### Part 2: Our Story (Real Photos)
- Editorial chapter timeline with luxury arched photo frames: Roka, Engagement, and Moments Together.
- Retro Childhood Save-the-Date feature card celebrating the couple's earliest memories and milestones.

### Part 3: Playful Wedding Fun
- **Baraatis vs Ladkiwale Squad Selector**: Interactive squad badge picker with persistent selection (`localStorage`).
- **Joota Chhupai Game**: Interactive shoe hunt for Ankur's golden jutti with a celebratory petal shower and shagun ransom popup.

### Part 4: Celebrations, Venues & RSVP
- Complete cards for all 5 wedding functions with Google Maps links and one-click `.ics` calendar file downloads.
- Direct WhatsApp RSVP buttons connected to family coordinators Vanshika & Ameer.
